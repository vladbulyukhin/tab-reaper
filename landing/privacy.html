The short version

Tab Reaper uses tab information to remove duplicates, close inactive tabs and protect tabs you choose to keep. We do not operate a server that receives your browsing history, and the extension has no advertising or analytics SDK.

This policy covers Tab Reaper, its Safari container app and this website. Features vary by platform and version; the upcoming Safari release includes persistent timers and optional saved history that may not yet be available in the published Chrome version.

Information used by the extension

The extension reads browser-provided tab information, including URLs, titles, tab and window identifiers, activity and properties such as pinned or audible status. It uses this information to apply your cleanup rules.

  • Preferences and protected sites: stored using browser sync storage with a local fallback. If browser-account sync is enabled, your browser provider may synchronize preferences and protected-site hostnames under its own privacy policy.
  • Recently closed tabs: URLs, titles, favicon references, closure times and reasons are stored locally or for the browser session. Newer versions retain up to 50 entries. Remembering history across restarts is optional and can be disabled; the list can be cleared.
  • Timers and “Keep this tab”: newer versions keep local timer and protection metadata, including salted URL hashes and tab-context fingerprints, to restore state after restarts. These are not described as anonymous data.
  • Diagnostics: when enabled, a bounded local journal records cleanup events. Disabling diagnostics stops logging and clears the journal. A report may contain sensitive tab details; copying or sending it is your choice.

Tab Reaper does not read page contents through content scripts. Browser-supplied favicon URLs may cause requests to the sites serving those icons.

Sharing and service providers

Tab Reaper does not automatically upload cleanup history, timer records or diagnostic reports to us. Browser sync is handled by your browser provider. Apple handles App Store purchases and distribution; we do not receive your payment-card details.

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Support sent to the currently listed Gmail address is processed by Google and your own email provider. Please remove private URLs and other sensitive information from reports before sharing them. Public GitHub issues are visible to other people.

External providers may process information outside your country or the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices describe processing locations and any applicable transfer safeguards. We do not sell support messages or browsing information.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement.

The website uses your device’s built-in fonts and does not load fonts, scripts or other resources from third parties. The interactive demo on the landing page runs entirely in your browser; nothing you do in it is stored or sent anywhere. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies to this website. External links, such as the Chrome Web Store, take you to services with their own policies.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until replaced, cleared, removed by the extension or deleted with its browser storage. Use the history clear button, history preference and diagnostics toggle where available. Removing a protected site updates the stored list; browser sync may propagate that change.

You can revoke extension access, disable browser sync or remove the extension and its data through your browser. Uninstalling an Apple container app may not clear all Safari extension data; use Safari’s extension and website-data controls as needed. Removing local data does not delete emails already sent to support.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information we hold, object to processing, and complain to your local data-protection authority. We process support messages to respond to your request and maintain support, and legal records when required by law.
