Using Tab Reaper

Tab Reaper is developed by Vladislav Bulyukhin. These terms describe use of this website and distributed Tab Reaper apps. Use the product lawfully and choose cleanup settings that suit your workflow.

Tab Reaper can close tabs automatically. Protect important tabs, pause cleanup when needed, and save work before enabling rules. Recently closed recovery reopens a URL; it cannot guarantee restoration of forms, unsaved edits, login state or page position.

Browser limits and availability

Behavior depends on the browser, permissions, tab visibility and operating system. A suspended browser or extension may delay cleanup. Safari tab groups that are not exposed to the extension cannot be cleaned until they become available. Matching restored tabs is conservative and cannot guarantee every timer or protection survives every browser change.

We aim to keep Tab Reaper useful and reliable, but cannot promise uninterrupted operation, compatibility with every browser release or recovery of lost work. Updates may change supported features.
