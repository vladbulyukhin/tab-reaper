How do I install it?

Chrome: install from the Chrome Web Store, then pin Tab Reaper from the extensions menu for easy access.

Safari: the App Store release is coming soon. Once available, install the app and follow its setup instructions. On Mac, enable it under Safari → Settings → Extensions. On recent iOS versions, go to Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions; older versions use Settings → Safari → Extensions. Open the extension from Safari’s extensions menu.

Which tabs stay open?

Active tabs are not idle-cleanup candidates. Use “Keep this tab” for a particular tab or protect a site to keep its tabs out of cleanup. Pinned, grouped and audible-tab handling depends on your settings and browser support. Pause cleanup whenever you need time to work.

The tab-count threshold applies to idle cleanup per browser window, not to duplicate removal. Check the options offered by your installed version.

Will timers run while Safari is closed?

iOS can suspend Safari and its extension, so cleanup cannot run continuously while the browser is unavailable. Newer versions save idle timing state and reconcile eligible tabs when Safari resumes. A tab may close after you return, rather than at the exact deadline while your phone is locked.

Only tabs made visible through the browser’s extension APIs can be managed. Other Safari tab groups may be handled when you switch back to them. Restoring ambiguous tab identities is conservative to avoid closing the wrong tab.

Can I get a closed tab back?

Open Tab Reaper’s recently closed list and select the tab to reopen its URL. This is not a backup of page contents or unsaved work. Newer versions offer an optional “remember across restarts” setting and a clear-list button. Turning off remembering prevents that history from being retained across browser restarts.

Why didn’t a tab close?

Check whether cleanup is paused, the tab or site is protected, the window is below your idle threshold, or pinned/grouped/audible handling excludes it. A timeout change starts a new idle interval in newer versions. Safari may need time to resume its background extension.

Enable diagnostics in extension settings, reproduce the issue, and review the report. Disable diagnostics again if you do not want local event logging. If the issue persists, contact support with the relevant details.
