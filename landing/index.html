Small tool. Thoughtful rules.

A lighter browser.
Not a blank slate.

Just enough automation to make space. Just enough control to keep it yours.

Idle cleanup

Choose how long inactive tabs stay open. The idle threshold applies separately to each browser window.

Duplicate removal

Keep one useful copy. Remove repeated pages without waiting for the idle threshold.

Protected tabs and sites

Keep one tab, or protect a whole site. Choose how pinned, grouped and audible tabs are treated.

Pause controls

In the middle of something? Pause cleanup and resume when you’re ready.

Recently closed recovery

Reopen a URL from your recently closed list. Remembering the list across restarts is optional; unsaved page edits aren’t restored.

Restart-aware timers

Saved timing and protection state helps newer versions resume cleanup after browser restarts. On iOS, cleanup can catch up when Safari returns.

Private by design

Your tabs are your business.

Tab cleanup runs on your device. We don’t receive your browsing history.

No Tab Reaper account. Your browser may sync preferences and protected sites through its own account service.Read our privacy policy ↗
